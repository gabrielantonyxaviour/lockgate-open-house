Rehearse without changing the deal

Rehearsal mode tells you where to stop before signing or confirming. Reading pages and filling forms is repeatable. Registration, funding, settlement, mandate updates, repayment and withdrawal change real testnet state.

For the complete recorded run, start the recorder before the first wallet action. Keep the same Alder holding, Meridian vehicle and resulting deal throughout. Do not repeat a completed payment just to obtain another take.

Optional extra clips and failure paths

Institutional enquiry: from Add New Profile or the entry cards, open 02 / 03 → Talk to us. Fill a clearly marked TEST enquiry, click Review enquiry and capture that screen. Do not send unless you want a real enquiry submitted.

Saved agreements: before signing, choose Save & agree later. Open Agreements → Saved for later → View / export saved draft → Resume review. Expired terms use Review current terms. Record this as a separate extra, then return to the live deal and verify its current amount and expiry.

Wrong identity: test only with a separate disposable test wallet; do not replace Lucas’s bound identity during the recorded deal.

Withdrawal queue: only record queue processing if the real withdrawal queues. 03 can Process withdrawal queue; 04 can Check queue and Claim when ready. Never edit a queued request into a confirmed payment.
