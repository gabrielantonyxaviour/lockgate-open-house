When the observed result differs

  • Wait for confirmed: a signature, approval, submitted hash or queue request is not a completed payment. Keep recording the actual state.
  • Wrong offer: do not sign a purchase/assignment offer for this story. Use Meridian’s Financed discharge offer for the 20,000-unit slice.
  • Reservation interrupted: return to the investor dashboard, refresh and use Reserved exits → Release reservation for the unfinished attempt. Wait for confirmation before requesting a replacement offer.
  • Changed balances or amounts: stop that clip and retain it. Note the actual receipt and tell us before continuing. The target arithmetic below assumes one fresh 1,000 subscription and this one exit/repayment.
  • Identity mismatch or queue testing: keep these as separate optional clips on separate test accounts. Do not replace the hero provider with a seeded large account or splice a queued payment into this cash-available withdrawal.
