Open Lockgate

1. Connect and sign in

Use your installed wallet on public Arbitrum Sepolia. Sign the wallet sign-in message, then choose your workspace. Arbitrum One has no financial deployment.

2. Prepare your connected wallet

Open Demo in the top bar. Get test gas if your wallet has no Sepolia ETH. For 01 or 04, choose one of the preverified demo identities and sign its wallet-binding transaction. These choices are test identity records; your actual wallet remains connected.

3. Test the investor or capital-provider flow

01: Use a matching identity, review its registered position, and mint a test position from Demo if needed. Request offers, review the net payout and agreement, sign, then settle using your wallet.

04: Use an eligible identity, get test USDG from Demo, choose a firm vehicle, sign exact subscription terms, approve spending, and deposit. Review your ownership and withdrawal options.

You can retain the same identity when switching between investor and capital-provider workspaces. Do not change identities between signing a subscription and depositing.

4. Review and record

Check balances, receipts and agreements in Records. Ordinary wallets use Talk to us for 02 and 03; institutional actions require their already-approved organization wallets.
