# Exit investor

**Job:** receive an earlier payout for a supported investment already owned. Primary service is early exit. Ordinary originator redemption is background context or a fallback link, never mandatory new-investment onboarding or the main task. Current entry follows [wallet/profile routing](entry-and-build-order.md): Start KYC precedes position discovery and trusted ownership matching. Exactly ten preverified TEST profiles exercise eight matches, one verified wrong owner and one verified empty account. Proposed journey; partial current capabilities are labelled. [Shared rules R1–R6](README.md#rules-applying-to-every-screen-row) apply to every row.

**Rights:** only the investor's matched holding/request may be offered. A complete exit changes those rights according to route A or B; a partial exit changes only a legally and operationally divisible slice. Partial loan-claim assignment needs its own approval. The investor is not the ordinary borrower under route B; any applicable warranties, indemnities or exceptional recourse must appear in the reviewed documents. An originator or firm cannot use persona selection as the investor's authorization.

## Required agreement roles

Templates below are a **proposed document inventory requiring counsel approval**, not executable production terms. Formalities, acceptance and consent depend on the actual instrument, governing law and vehicle.

| Document | Who signs or accepts | Evidence before settlement |
|---|---|---|
| Electronic-signing consent and wallet-control binding | Investor or authorized entity representative; binding service verifies challenge | Consent version plus domain/nonce/expiry; wallet signature is not the exit agreement |
| Holding verification/data-sharing authorization | Investor consents; originator/registrar supplies authenticated record under its data arrangement | Scoped record/matching reference; no unnecessary PII on chain |
| A: sale/assignment instrument | Investor as seller/assignor; named purchasing vehicle as buyer/assignee through authorized firm representative | Executed exact asset/quantity and price; originator/registrar/obligor consent or notice where instrument requires it |
| B: settlement and release agreement | Investor as old creditor/holder; actual old obligor as settling party; originator signs only if it is that obligor or authorized agent; financing vehicle joins if duties require | Release tied to successful conditional payment, old claim ID/slice and residual rights; separate borrower financing agreement is listed in shared contract |
| Settlement instruction/receipt | Investor authorizes destination; settlement operator/registrar certifies its leg | Payout address/account, settled quantity, document version, transfer/discharge evidence |
