# Originating platform

**Job:** let eligible existing investors obtain earlier exits under an approved route, with accurate holdings, protected settlement and an operationally serviced obligation. Proposed journey; local planning and several issuer controls already exist. [Shared rules R1–R6](README.md#rules-applying-to-every-screen-row) apply to every row.

**Authority:** the platform may be an operator, fund, registrar, borrower or authorized agent; those roles must be named separately. Neither a technical issuer wallet nor an originator logo establishes borrowing authority, ownership of investor claims or commercial approval.

**Entry:** 02 uses **Talk to us** for team-led partnership onboarding. Invited
representatives complete assigned evidence, agreements and integration tasks online;
approved authenticated profiles open their assigned platform dashboard.

Enquiry entry collects representative name, work email, organization, jurisdiction and
a short role-specific summary. An actual received enquiry returns a durable reference
and branded acknowledgement email; receipt grants no approval. The local preview
shows the form and an unsent email only. [Detailed contract](identity-enquiries-and-syndication.md).

## Proposed agreement inventory

All templates and required formalities require counsel approval before production use.

| Document | Actual party roles | Evidence required |
|---|---|---|
| Organization authority and onboarding declarations | Originator authorized representative; actual borrower separately if different | Entity, delegated powers, signer roster, scope/expiry and reviewer decision |
| Integration/data/servicing agreement | Originator/platform operator; actual registrar/servicer; Lockgate operator for its contracted technology duties; vehicle where it relies on records | Data duties, investor consent basis, lock/discharge authority, outages, disputes, retention and reconciliation responsibility |
| A: transfer/assignment consent or acknowledgement | Seller investor and purchasing vehicle execute transaction; fund/issuer/obligor/registrar signs consent or acknowledges notice when required | Instrument-specific permission; transfer registration, settlement conditions and future servicing beneficiary |
| B: borrower financing facility and drawdown | Actual borrower; named funding vehicle as lender through authorized representative; guarantor/security provider where applicable | Principal, due amount, pricing, currency, maturity, draw conditions, priority and enforcement; no implied debt against platform operator |
| B: old-claim settlement/release | Investor and actual old obligor; platform acts only in documented capacity | Conditional payment, released slice, residual rights, discharge evidence and failed-leg resolution |
| Reserve/security/priority documents | Owner of reserve/security; beneficiary/lender or security trustee; relevant existing creditor if required | Ownership, segregation, perfection/consents where applicable, permitted use/release and waterfall |
