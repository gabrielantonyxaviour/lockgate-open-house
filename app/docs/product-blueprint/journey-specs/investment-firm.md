# Licensed investment firm

**Job:** manage an authorized vehicle, underwrite originating platforms and instruments, commit available capital to suitable exits, and service providers and obligations. “Licensed” describes a requirement to verify for the actual activity/jurisdiction; a wallet cannot select or create licence scope. [Shared rules R1–R6](README.md#rules-applying-to-every-screen-row) apply to every row.

**Parties:** management company, purchasing/lending vehicle, subscription issuer, custodian, administrator and security trustee may differ. Every investment identifies its actual purchasing/lending vehicle. Provider economic ownership is independent of the manager's operational keys.

**Entry:** 03 uses **Talk to us**. Onboarding is team-led: review, negotiation,
agreements and governed readiness/activation. Invited representatives complete
assigned tasks online; approved authenticated wallets open the assigned dashboard.

## Proposed agreement inventory

Counsel must approve every production template, party, formal requirement and applicability.

| Document | Signatory/acceptance roles |
|---|---|
| Vehicle formation/management mandate | Vehicle's authorized governing body and management company; administrator/custodian separately for their duties |
| Lockgate operating/technology agreement | Management company and/or vehicle actually buying the service; Lockgate service entity; data/operational counterparties as relevant |
| Provider offering/subscription package | Provider or authorized representative; actual issuer/vehicle; firm accepts only under documented authority; management company signs its own obligations if any |
| Originator underwriting/facility/security package | Actual borrower, vehicle as lender, security owner/trustee and required consenting creditor; never substitute originator brand for legal obligor |
| A purchase/assignment | Investor seller/assignor and vehicle buyer/assignee; issuer/registrar/obligor consent or notice where required |
| B drawdown plus settlement/release | Borrower and vehicle for new debt; investor and old obligor for discharge; any agent's authority explicitly recorded |
| Valuation, withdrawal, recovery and governance policies | Authorized vehicle/firm governing body approves; providers acknowledge/consent where their terms require; operational delegates accept assigned duties |

Proposed risk default, **not decided**: assess originator/related-group exposure using outstanding plus reserved face exposure over dated provider net asset value, with separate absolute loss and cash limits. NAV impairment can increase this ratio; stop new commitments on breach while preserving servicing. Reserve coverage is a separate reserve-asset value / covered exposure ratio. Final denominator and treatment of acquired units, FX and contingent obligations require approval (`D-RISK`).
