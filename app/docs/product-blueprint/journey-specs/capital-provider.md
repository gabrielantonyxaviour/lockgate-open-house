# Liquidity investor / capital provider

**Job:** invest through an approved firm-managed vehicle, understand personal ownership and risk, then receive proceeds under its liquidity policy. This is a proposed product; existing PartnerVault is owner-funded and cannot support this journey. [Shared rules R1–R6](README.md#rules-applying-to-every-screen-row) apply to every row.

**Provisional direction:** firm-approved providers receive individual shares/interests. Eligible withdrawal uses uncommitted idle liquidity or a queue. Legal wrapper, valuation, fees, loss/recovery rights and queue policies remain open. Provider rights never equal the manager's aggregate dashboard balance.

## Proposed agreement inventory

All production templates require counsel approval; a demo signs explicitly TEST documents and proves only the signing workflow.

| Document | Party / required evidence |
|---|---|
| Identity/data and electronic-signing consent | Provider or authorized representative; verification service/firm collects consent for documented purposes |
| Offering memorandum/risk disclosures | Issuer/vehicle supplies current document; provider acknowledges as required; no invented signature requirement for every disclosure |
| Subscription agreement | Provider subscribes; actual issuer/vehicle countersigns or separately accepts under its prescribed process; authorized firm signs only as representative or for its own duties |
| Investment/management terms | Provider and actual contracting entity where a bilateral agreement is required; identify how vehicle interests confer rights and who maintains the register |
| Custody/administration terms | Actual custodian/administrator and its contracting client where applicable; no promise that management company itself safeguards assets |
| Withdrawal, valuation, fees, loss/recovery and amendments | Included in executed package; versions/acknowledgements link to subscription; material amendments follow agreed consent policy |
| Wallet recovery/beneficiary instruction | Authenticated entitled provider and authorized approving role; change audit retained; no transfer of title from a profile edit |

Proposed queue default, **not decided**: publish NAV cutoff; unfilled queued shares continue sharing gains/losses, permit cancellation of unfilled shares, and reserve settled cash exclusively for fixed claims. Choose FIFO versus pro-rata and late-recovery beneficiary before implementation; neither is inferred from current contracts.

Current limitation: [owner-only deposits/withdrawals](../../../../contracts/src/partner/PartnerVault.sol) and [owner-only share attribution](../../../../contracts/src/partner/PartnerVaultRead.sol). A new provider layer requires individual ownership, pricing, queues and reconciliation; institutional lending to Lockgate's separate facility is not a substitute.
